About Me

My story

I started learning web development at CWW Africa because I want to build things people can use. Every page I write by hand teaches me how the web really works.

What I am practising

My focus right now is writing HTML and CSS that is clear, organised and welcoming to everyone.

Why accessibility matters to me

A good website works for all visitors, including people who use screen readers or keyboards. Headings in the right order, labelled form fields and meaningful alt text are small habits that make a big difference.

Where I am heading

Next, I plan to add interactivity with JavaScript, then build a small project I can share with others.
